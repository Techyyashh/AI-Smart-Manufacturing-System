How predictive maintenance works here
1. SenseMachine sensors stream temperature, vibration, speed, pressure and electrical data every second.
2. NormaliseEach reading is compared with that machine's own normal operating values.
3. PredictA Random Forest model (Python, scikit-learn) estimates the probability of failure.
4. ActAlerts, a health score and remaining useful life help schedule maintenance before a breakdown.
